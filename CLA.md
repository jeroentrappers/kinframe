## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA check will ask you to comment on it with this exact sentence:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub username, the time and the pull request are recorded in `signatures/cla.json` on the
`cla-signatures` branch. You sign once; it covers all your later contributions.
