kinframe becomes your family's private place to share.

Home: everything your families share, newest first, with the reactions from people and from the frames.

Comments: talk about every photo, reply, mention someone with @, and see Oma's ❤ from her frame right in the conversation.

Chats: one-to-one, groups, a chat for every family and for circles inside it. Send photos, videos and voice messages, and put any photo on a frame with one tap.

Families: "feeds" are now called families. Make circles for the cousins or a household, and add family members who only want to follow along.

Gentle reminders: see which frames are getting quiet, answer when Oma taps "Ask for new photos", keep the family streak going and try the weekly theme. Few reminders, never at night, easy to turn off.

Leaving a family: decide whether your photos stay or go with you. Frames are told and can ask to keep some. Leave people out of your memories, privately.

Several servers: sign in to more than one kinframe server and see all your families together. Frames can follow a family on another server.
