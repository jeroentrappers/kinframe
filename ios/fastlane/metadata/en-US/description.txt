Your photos, on their frame, in seconds.

kinframe sends photos and videos to the family's photo frames. No subscriptions, no tracking, and the server belongs to your family.

SEND FROM ANYWHERE Pick photos and videos in the app, or share them straight from Photos with the share sheet. They show up on the frame a few seconds later. Add a caption, send to several frames at once, and Live Photos come along too.

SEE THE SMILE You can see when a photo arrives and when it's been seen. Hearts and quick replies from the frame come right back to you.

MANAGE YOUR FRAMES Add a frame, invite the family, and decide who can send. Arrange albums, set when the frame turns its screen on and off, add calendars and widgets, or show a slideshow on your TV with AirPlay.

YOURS TO KEEP kinframe is open source and free software (AGPL-3.0), with no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. Use the hosted service at kinframe.appmire.be, with servers in Germany, or run your own server: the app works with both.

kinframe needs a kinframe photo frame: a Wi-Fi photo frame running the kinframe frame app.
