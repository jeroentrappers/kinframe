PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
kinframe sends photos and short videos from your phone to Wi-Fi photo frames in the homes of people you love, typically grandparents and other relatives who don't use smartphones. Families use it to share everyday moments: you pick photos, choose a frame and press Send; the frame shows them within seconds and you see when they were delivered and seen, and any reaction. Audience: adults sharing within their own family. There is no public feed, no strangers and no discovery: every frame is private and only people invited by its owner can send to it.

HOW TO REVIEW (no hardware needed)
1. Sign in with the demo account below. On the sign-in page choose "Use password" (e-mail codes are the default).
2. The account is a member of "Demo frame", a frame that is always online for App Review. It confirms what you send as delivered and seen and reacts with a heart.
3. Tap Send, pick photos, choose Demo frame and send. Follow the delivery in History.
4. To watch photos arrive on a screen, open https://kinframe.appmire.be/tv/frame in any web browser: it becomes a frame and shows a code. In the app choose Frames > Add frame, enter that code and send photos to it; they appear in the browser within seconds.
5. New accounts: enter any new e-mail address on the sign-in page and confirm the code sent to it, or use Sign in with Apple / Google.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete account. The account, profile photo and sign-in are deleted for good; optionally the photos sent too. The demo account is recreated after review.

USER CONTENT AND MODERATION
Content is only shared inside private, invite-only family frames. A sender can take back photos at any time (History > send > Remove now); they disappear from every frame. The frame owner and admins can remove any member (Frames > frame > People > member > Remove from frame, optionally "Also remove the photos they sent"); that person can no longer send to the frame unless invited again. Any member can leave a frame (Leave frame). Problems can be reported to jeroen@appmire.be; we respond within 24 hours.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Sign-in: Logto (self-hosted at auth.kinframe.appmire.be), Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In.
- Push notifications: Apple Push Notification service.
- Hosting and photo storage: our own servers at Hetzner (Germany, EU).
- Sign-in e-mails: our own mail server (mail.appmire.be).
- Optional frame widgets: Open-Meteo (weather), calendars the user connects (Google, Microsoft, iCloud).
No in-app purchases or payments, no ads, no analytics, no AI services.

REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. Its interface is available in English and Dutch.

REGULATION
kinframe does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected content; users only share their own photos.
