Give the old tablet in the drawer a second life: kinframe turns it into a photo frame for the living room, the kitchen or grandma's sideboard. The family sends photos and videos from their phones, and a few seconds later they're on the frame. No subscriptions, no ads, no tracking.

HOW IT WORKS
Install kinframe on the tablet, pick a language and Wi-Fi, and the frame shows an 8-digit code. Enter that code in the kinframe app on your phone (Android or iPhone) or on kinframe.appmire.be and the frame is yours. Invite the family, and everyone can send.

MADE TO RUN ALL DAY
• Make kinframe the tablet's home app and it starts by itself when the tablet turns on. The setup shows you how.
• Keep the tablet plugged in: kinframe keeps the screen on and darkens it at night on the sleep schedule you choose.
• Photos are kept on the tablet, so the slideshow keeps going when the Wi-Fi drops.
• An owner PIN keeps settings and Android's menus out of reach of little fingers.

MORE THAN A SLIDESHOW
• Captions, hearts and quick replies: a tap on the frame lets the sender know the photo made someone smile.
• Videos and live photos, with sound if you like.
• Albums, "on this day", a clock, the weather, calendars and countdowns, and birthday cards with balloons.
• Live widgets from your home: Home Assistant, HomeWizard and more.

YOURS TO KEEP
kinframe is open source and free software (AGPL-3.0). Use the hosted service at kinframe.appmire.be, with servers in Germany, or run your own kinframe server: the frame works with both. There are no ads, no analytics and no telemetry.

WHAT YOU NEED
• An Android tablet with Android 6.0 or newer and Wi-Fi.
• Someone in the family with the kinframe app or a web browser to claim the frame and send photos.
