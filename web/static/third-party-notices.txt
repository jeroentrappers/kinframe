kinframe is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later).
Source code: https://github.com/jeroentrappers/kinframe

This file lists the components that the web app ships as WebAssembly to prepare photos in your browser. They run
only on your own device, and only when needed (see "When they are loaded" below). Each is distributed under its own
licence, as listed here.


When they are loaded
--------------------

- The photo preparation module (kinframe_prep_bg.wasm) is loaded only when the browser can't decode or encode a
  photo itself.
- The HEIF module (libheif.wasm) is loaded only for HEIC/HEIF (and AVIF) photos that the browser can't decode
  itself. Safari decodes HEIC natively and never loads it.

HEVC, the image format inside HEIC files, is covered by patents in some countries. kinframe only decodes it, on the
user's own device, and only when the user picks such a photo. Operators who distribute kinframe are responsible for
checking whether a patent licence applies to them.
